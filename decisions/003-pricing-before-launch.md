# 003: The price grid is revised before launch so that the launch offer is the best deal

*Version française : [003-pricing-before-launch.fr.md](003-pricing-before-launch.fr.md)*

- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Status:** Adopted
- **Area:** pricing

## Context

A week before the public launch on 20 October, I reread the pricing page as a customer would. The "launch offer" was a monthly Pass at €49, kept while the subscription stays active, against a regular €59 monthly or €390 yearly. Over a year, the offer cost €588, more than the regular yearly Pass. The offer was the most expensive plan on the page, so it created no reason to act. Nobody had subscribed to it yet, which made a change free of side effects. Two other facts weighed in: a course sold at €39 felt high for a first purchase from a new platform, and one course (an AI coding project) was free for every account, on top of the free course each person chooses.

## Options

| Option | For | Against |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A. Keep the grid (€39 course, €59 monthly, €49 launch, €390 yearly) | No work | The offer stays more expensive than the yearly plan: no urgency, and a page that looks inconsistent |
| B. Lower everything for a "year one" grid (€29 course, €29 monthly, €190 yearly), raised for new customers in year two | Attractive, simple | Anchors a low value for the Pass; a large increase later is harder to justify; no launch moment |
| C. Regular Pass at €39 monthly or €390 yearly ("2 months free"), launch offer at €29 monthly until 31 October kept while active, course at €19, paths from €59 to €169 | The launch offer (€348 over a year) beats the yearly plan; the yearly plan is a real saving against the monthly; a course is cheap enough to try a second one | Lower revenue per course; paths had to be repriced so each stays cheaper than its courses bought one by one |

## Decision

Option C, because it is the only grid where each plan has a clear reason to exist: the launch offer is the best deal until 31 October, the yearly plan saves two months against the monthly one, and a single course is an easy second purchase. The always-free course is removed: every account gets exactly one free course, chosen by the learner.

| | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Course, lifetime access | €19 |
| Learning path, lifetime access | €59 to €169 (always below its courses bought one by one) |
| Pass, monthly | €39 (€29 for subscriptions taken by 31 October 2026, kept while active) |
| Pass, yearly | €390, "2 months free" compared with the €39 monthly plan |
| First course | Free in full, chosen by the learner |

## Consequences

- No struck-through price and no "-x %": "2 months free" compares two current plans (12 × €39 = €468), never a former price.
- Prices will rise when the catalogue and the platform have grown, and each increase will have to be justified by what it adds. An ongoing subscription keeps its price, and a course or path already bought is never charged again. This is written in the terms of sale.
- What I watch: the share of free-course learners who buy something, and the share of paths among purchases. Both will appear in [metrics](../metrics/README.md), monthly for usage and quarterly for revenue.
- Accounts created before this decision keep the course that used to be free only if they used it, chose it or bought it.
