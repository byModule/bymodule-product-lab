# 003: The price grid is revised before launch so that the launch offer is the best deal

*Version française : [003-pricing-before-launch.fr.md](003-pricing-before-launch.fr.md)*

- **Date:** 2026-10-12
- **Status:** Adopted
- **Area:** pricing

## Context

Before the public launch on 20 October, the price grid had to follow the value each plan delivers. Every course ends with a deliverable on the learner's own product (a spec, an evaluation plan, an AI policy), so a single course is priced like one deliverable. The grid also had to offer three clear ways to buy: à la carte for one specific need, a full-access subscription for those who want the whole catalogue, and a yearly payment that genuinely saves money. Learning paths come on top, for Product Managers who want to progress on one theme across several courses.

The grid in place at the time did not pass that test. The "launch offer" was a monthly Pass at €49, kept while the subscription stays active, against a regular €59 monthly or €390 yearly: over a year the offer cost €588, more than the regular yearly Pass, so it gave no reason to act. A course at €39 was high for a first purchase on a new platform, and one course (an AI coding project) was free for every account on top of the free course each person chooses. Nobody had subscribed to the launch offer yet, so a change had no side effects.

## Options

| Option | For | Against |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A. Keep the grid (€39 course, €59 monthly, €49 launch, €390 yearly) | No work | The offer stays more expensive than the yearly plan: no urgency, and a page that looks inconsistent |
| B. Lower everything for a "year one" grid (€29 course, €29 monthly, €190 yearly), raised for new customers in year two | Attractive, simple | Anchors a low value for the Pass; a large increase later is harder to justify; no launch moment |
| C. Regular Pass at €39 monthly or €390 yearly ("2 months free"), launch offer at €29 monthly until 31 October kept while active, course at €19, paths from €59 to €169 | The launch offer (€348 over a year) beats the yearly plan; the yearly plan is a real saving against the monthly; a course is cheap enough to try a second one | Lower revenue per course; paths had to be repriced so each stays cheaper than its courses bought one by one |

## Decision

Option C, because it is the only grid where each plan has a clear reason to exist and a price in line with what it delivers: a single course for one deliverable, a path for a theme, the Pass for the whole catalogue, the yearly plan to save two months, and the launch offer as the best deal until 31 October inclusive. The always-free course is removed: every account gets exactly one free course, chosen by the learner.

| | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Course, lifetime access | €19 |
| Learning path, lifetime access | €59 to €169 by theme (AI Product Builder, Technical PM, AI-Augmented PM, Solo Builder, AI Every Day), always below its courses bought one by one |
| Pass, monthly | €39 (€29 for subscriptions taken by 31 October 2026 inclusive, kept while active) |
| Pass, yearly | €390, "2 months free" compared with the €39 monthly plan |
| First course | Free in full, chosen by the learner |

## Consequences

- No struck-through price and no "-x %": "2 months free" compares two current plans (12 × €39 = €468), never a former price.
- Prices are guaranteed at subscription: an ongoing subscription keeps the price it was taken at, and a course or path already bought is never charged again. Prices for new customers will rise when the catalogue and the platform have grown, and each increase will have to be justified by what it adds. This is written in the terms of sale.
- What I watch: the share of free-course learners who buy something, and the share of paths among purchases. Both will appear in [metrics](../metrics/README.md), monthly for usage and quarterly for revenue.
- Accounts created before this decision keep the course that used to be free only if they used it, chose it or bought it.
