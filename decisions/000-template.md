# NNN: Title of the decision (one sentence, present tense)

- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Status:** Proposed · Adopted · Reviewed on YYYY-MM-DD · Abandoned
- **Area:** content · platform · pricing · communication

## Context

Three to five sentences. What question came up, at what point in the project, under which constraints (time, budget, skills, what already existed). The figures that mattered at that moment, with their source.

## Options

| Option | For | Against |

| A | | |
| B | | |

## Decision

What was chosen and, in one sentence, the criterion that settled it.

## Consequences

- What it makes possible.
- What it costs or closes off.
- What to watch to know whether it was the right call, and when to look.

## Review

*Empty when the decision is written. Filled in when the decision is confirmed, adjusted or abandoned, with the date and what prompted the review (figures, cited feedback).*
