# Changelog

*Version française : [CHANGELOG.fr.md](CHANGELOG.fr.md)*

One line per delivery to the live Module learning platform ([bymodule.com](https://bymodule.com)), newest first, since this lab was published. Dates are production release dates. Small fixes are grouped; internal tooling is not listed. What is planned lives in the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md).

## October 2026

- **2026-10-29** · Every course now requires a 10-question final assessment for its certificate.
- **2026-10-26** · A new « AI courses for Product Managers » page brings the AI courses together, and the glossary gains two entries: LLM and prompt.
- **2026-10-19** · The site has a fresh look: bolder typography, a shorter and more direct home page, and an identity aligned with the Module studio.
- **2026-10-19** · At the end of your free course, you can share your feedback in two questions.
- **2026-10-13** · A What's new page lists every change to the platform, by release date.
- **2026-10-12** · English URLs for English lesson pages, with permanent redirects from the former addresses.
- **2026-10-12** · Public lesson pages show the outcomes of their module instead of the whole course; the lesson pages of five courses also show the questions each lesson answers (first batch).
- **2026-10-08** · Search fixes: the onboarding page is prerendered for search engines, five lessons get distinct titles, and the navigation menu loads its full content only when first opened.
- **2026-10-06** · Administration: an audience page that reads Google Analytics (read only). Fix: analytics hits from bymodule.com were blocked by the content security policy until this release.
- **2026-10-06** · New course announcement email. A course added to a learning path is granted to the path's past buyers, and the terms of sale say so.
- **2026-10-05** · Learner notification centre: mark as read or unread, delete, filter.
- **2026-10-05** · Every course follows the same integration (onboarding recommendation, certificates, checkout, catalogue visibility), checked by a test that runs on every course.
- **2026-10-05** · Administration: a view of cancellation reasons, and cancellation flow events in analytics. Two-step verification becomes mandatory for staff accounts.
- **2026-10-04** · One layout for every platform email. Learners stay signed in after sign-up and land on their course after payment. The logo in the dashboard and the course player leads back to the dashboard home.
- **2026-10-03** · Lifecycle emails at key moments of the learner journey, including a reminder before the launch offer ends.
- **2026-10-03** · In-app cancellation with offers: a short survey, a pause of one to three months, a free month, alternatives to the Pass, and reactivation.
- **2026-10-03** · Account settings for password change and notification preferences; a denser help centre with direct links.
- **2026-10-03** · Valid course rich results and unique page titles for search engines; anti-abuse hardening.
- **2026-10-02** · Redesign: a technical-drawing visual language across the home page, public pages, dashboard and course player.
- **2026-10-02** · Staff roles and two-step verification for staff accounts.
- **2026-10-01** · Certificates for courses and learning paths: issued, shareable on LinkedIn, verifiable, with five named levels.
- **2026-10-01** · Public resource hub with filters; the glossary becomes a Library page in the dashboard.
- **2026-10-01** · Public glossary of 31 Product Management concepts, in French and English.
- **2026-10-01** · Public lesson previews for the 22 courses that did not have them yet.
- **2026-10-01** · Course time in the player reflects real on-screen time.
- **2026-10-01** · Security hardening across authentication and backend services, including a 10-character minimum password. Dashboard polish.
