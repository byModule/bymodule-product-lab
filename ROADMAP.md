# Roadmap

*Version française : [ROADMAP.fr.md](ROADMAP.fr.md)*

Last reviewed: 2026-10-19

This roadmap covers the Module learning platform and this Product Lab. **Now** is what I am working on, **Next** is what I intend to start within the following months, **Later** is direction rather than commitment. Dates are targets, not promises. Each item carries one of the status labels defined in the [README](README.md#how-to-read-the-status-labels).

## Now (October to November 2026)

| Item | Status | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Build in public launch.** This repository gains a roadmap, a changelog, decision records, playbooks and a metrics page, and Discussions open for feedback. | In progress | October 2026 |
| **New course: "Using AI in product discovery".** Like every course, in French and English and ending with a deliverable. | In progress | 25 November 2026 |
| **Monthly metrics.** One usage file per month in [metrics](metrics/README.md), the first one covering October. | Planned | 2 November 2026 |

## Next (December 2026 to February 2027)

| Item | Status | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **January courses.** The subject of one of them is chosen by a LinkedIn poll on 10 November 2026; the result will be reported here. | Planned | January 2027 |
| **Interviews with learners.** Short conversations with people who signed up, to understand where they stop and why. An anonymised synthesis will be published here. | Planned | December 2026 |

## How to influence this roadmap

- **Discussions.** Propose a course in *Course ideas*, report a problem or an idea in *Platform feedback*, ask about the approach in *Questions about the method*: [Discussions](https://github.com/byModule/bymodule-product-lab/discussions).
- **On the platform.** A short feedback form at the end of the free course is planned; this line will link to it once it is live on [bymodule.com](https://bymodule.com).

When feedback changes this roadmap, I say so at the next review and cite the Discussion it came from (with the author's agreement).

## Review rhythm

I review this file every month. Items move between sections, change status, or leave the roadmap; the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md) records what was actually delivered.
